Accessibility and Usability

Accessible websites are designed so people with different abilities and devices can use them. Accessibility is also part of good mobile design.

Readable Text

Text should have enough color contrast, clear font sizes, and good spacing. This helps users on small screens and users with vision difficulties.

Keyboard and Touch

Navigation links, buttons, and quiz controls should be easy to select with a keyboard, mouse, or touch screen.

Alternative Text

Images include alt text so screen readers can explain the image content to users who cannot see it.
